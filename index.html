Why is ELSS better than PPF?

You: Should I invest in ELSS or PPF?
Finance Friend: ELSS has a shorter lock-in (3 years) and potential for higher returns, but PPF is safer.
You: What about taxes?
Finance Friend: Both give 80C benefit, but ELSS returns are taxed as LTCG after ₹1 lakh.
You: Which is better for me?
Finance Friend: If you want higher growth and can take some risk, try ELSS. For safety, pick PPF!
